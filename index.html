Round 1 · Whole-class warm-up

GUESS THE PRODUCT 🕵️

One student receives a secret card privately. Study the emojis and hints. You have 20 seconds to prepare. Describe the product without saying its name. The class has to guess!

Remember: Say what it is, what it looks like, what it does, where you use it, or why people need it.
Two examples for the class

Watch how it works

🎧📱🎵

Example A

Hint 1: You use it to listen to music.

Hint 2: You can connect it to your phone.

“You wear these on your head or in your ears. You use them to listen to music, videos, or podcasts. Some of them are wireless.”
Answer: Headphones
📸🤳📱

Example B

Hint 1: You use it to take pictures of yourself.

Hint 2: You usually use the front camera.

“It is a type of photo. You take it with your smartphone. People often share it on social media.”
Answer: A selfie

Round 3 · Team challenge

BUILD YOUR SALES PITCH 💡

Work in your groups. Finish the advertisement you started yesterday. Prepare a short presentation that makes people want to buy your product.

Introduce it

Say the product name and what it is.

Describe it

Give at least two details or features.

Make it attractive

Use at least three positive adjectives.

Talk about the deal

Say the condition and the price.

Convince buyers

Finish with a persuasive sentence.

Your presentation planner

Complete the model

Use this template to organize your ideas. Write keywords first, then turn them into complete sentences.

Complete example · Wireless headphones

“Hi, everyone! We are selling these wireless headphones. They have excellent sound quality and connect easily to your smartphone using Bluetooth. They are stylish, comfortable, and lightweight. They are in excellent condition and work perfectly. The original price was $60, but we are selling them for only $40. They are a great deal for music lovers. Don't miss this opportunity. Message us if you're interested!”

Round 4 · After each presentation

THE BUYER QUESTION WHEEL 🎡

After your team presents, spin the wheel and answer three different questions about your product. Give complete answers and add details. Your classmates can ask one follow-up question.

QUESTION 1 · 3 QUESTIONS TO ANSWER
What makes this product special?

Explain one feature that makes people interested.

Keep talking: Give a reason, an example, or an extra detail. Don't answer with only “yes” or “no”.

More ways to answer

The best thing about it is…We recommend it because…It is useful for…Compared to other products…You can get it for…We think it is a good choice because…
